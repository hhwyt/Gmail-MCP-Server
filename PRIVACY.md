# Privacy Policy

This is a personal, self-hosted Gmail MCP server. It runs only on the owner's own computer.

- **What it accesses:** Gmail messages, labels and filters of the Google accounts the owner signs in with, only when the owner's local AI assistant calls a tool.
- **Where data goes:** Requests go directly from the owner's computer to Google's Gmail API. No data is sent to the author or any other third party, and nothing is collected, sold or shared.
- **Storage:** OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's macOS keychain. Email content is not stored, except attachments the owner explicitly downloads to a local folder.
- **Revoking access:** Remove the app at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
